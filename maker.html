WHY RU MOTION

The motion you repeat should take one click.

Ru Motion is a motion toolkit for After Effects that I build, test and look after myself. The idea is simple: the animations you reach for again and again, such as a clean pop, a typewriter or a swipe cut, should not cost twenty minutes of keyframes every time.

It is made around how editors really work. You select a layer, click an effect, and it starts at the playhead. What you get is ordinary keyframes and expressions, so you can retime or change it afterwards, and one undo takes it back.

It keeps growing with real feedback. If something breaks or a feature is missing, tell me.
