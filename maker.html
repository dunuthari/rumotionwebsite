MY STORY

Why I built Ru Motion

Here’s what I noticed. The same animations came back in almost every project. A pop here, a typewriter there, a swipe cut. Every time, it took twenty minutes of keyframes. Every time.

And when I looked for plugins to fix it, most of them were paid. I didn’t want to pay again and again for things I could make myself. So I made Ru Motion.

Now it’s a free panel for After Effects. Select a layer, click an effect, and it starts right at the playhead. That’s it.

THE DETAIL

Why it has to be right

The part you don’t see is the part I check first.

When you click an effect, you get the keyframes behind it, not just the result. If I wouldn’t want to retime it myself at midnight before a deadline, it isn’t ready.

THE PROMISE

What you get, and what I won’t do

Normal keyframes and expressions. Nothing hidden. Drag them, change them, delete them, and one undo takes it all back.

It’s free. No subscription, and it stays free. Use it for personal or client work.

It doesn’t collect any data. It’s a tool, not a tracker.

If something breaks or you want a new feature, tell me. I read every message.
